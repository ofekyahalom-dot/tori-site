Straight answers

No fine print.

Does Tori collect my data?

No. Everything Tori does happens on your iPhone. There are no servers collecting your activity, no analytics, and no third-party trackers. The apps you lock and your study progress never leave your device.

Does it really use Apple's Screen Time?

Yes — Tori is built on Apple's Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity frameworks. iOS asks for your explicit permission before Tori can shield any app, and you can withdraw it at any time.

Does Tori watch what I do in other apps?

No. Tori only knows whether an app you chose is currently shielded or unlocked. It can't read your messages, your content, or your activity inside any other app — Apple's framework only hands it opaque tokens, not identities.

Can I unlock without studying?

Tori never traps you. You can disable its Screen Time permission in iOS Settings whenever you want — it's your phone. Tori just makes the next scroll cost a few minutes of Japanese first.

Is there an account or subscription?

No account. You open the app and it works. Any future paid features will be spelled out in the App Store listing before you buy.
