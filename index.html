How it works

The feed costs a few minutes of Japanese.

Tori sits between you and the apps you can't put down — and turns the urge to scroll into reps on your vocabulary.

1

Choose your distractions

Pick the apps that pull you in. Tori shields them using Apple's Screen Time controls — with permission you grant in iOS.

2

Hit a wall

Open a shielded app and you'll see Tori's lock screen instead of the feed.

3

Clear today's deck

Open Tori, finish your daily Japanese flashcards, then re-drill the cards you got wrong.

4

Earn your window

Finish and Tori unlocks the app for ten minutes. Then it locks again — the next scroll costs another deck.

Private by design

Your data never leaves your iPhone.

Tori runs entirely on your device. It uses Apple's Family Controls and Screen Time APIs — which you authorize yourself in iOS — to lock and unlock the apps you choose. We never see any of it.

Read the full privacy policy
  • No account, no login. Tori works the moment you open it — there's nothing to sign up for.
  • No tracking or analytics. No third-party SDKs, no advertising identifiers, no behavior collection.
  • Nothing leaves the device. Your decks, progress and locked-app choices stay on your iPhone.
  • You stay in control. Revoke Screen Time access anytime in iOS Settings. It's your device.
Questions

The honest answers.

Does Tori collect my data?

No. Everything Tori does happens on your iPhone. It has no servers collecting your activity, no analytics, and no third-party trackers. The apps you lock and your study progress never leave your device.

Does it really use Apple's Screen Time?

Yes — Tori is built on Apple's Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity frameworks. iOS asks for your explicit permission before Tori can shield any app, and you can withdraw that permission at any time.

Who is Tori for?

Anyone trying to break the doom-scroll and build a daily learning habit. You decide which apps get locked and what's in your deck. Right now Tori ships with Japanese vocabulary.

Can I unlock an app without studying?

Tori never traps you. You can disable its Screen Time permission in iOS Settings whenever you want — it's your phone. Tori simply makes the next scroll cost a few minutes of Japanese first.

Does Tori monitor what I do in other apps?

No. Tori only knows whether a chosen app is currently shielded or unlocked. It does not read your messages, your content, or your activity inside any other app.

Is there an account or subscription?

There's no account. You open the app and it works. Any future paid features will be made clear in the App Store listing before you buy.
